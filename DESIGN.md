# MiniBrick visual direction

A warm editorial gift experience led by the physical white shadow-box frame. Large confident typography, ample off-white space, deep red used for hearts and decisive actions, fine warm-gray dividers, and restrained monospace details echoing personal frame inscriptions. Product representations have tangible depth and quiet lighting; the configurator is the focal interaction.

## Tokens
- Red: #e9232d
- Dark: #171514
- Warm ground: #fbf8f4
- Cream: #f4eee7
- Muted: #706964
- Line: #e7dfd7
- White: #ffffff

## Principles
- Show the frame before abstract explanation.
- Use a clear split between editable controls and live result.
- Keep accents purposeful and avoid visual noise.
- Let occasion visuals vary while maintaining one consistent frame construction.
