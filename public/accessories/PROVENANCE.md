# Accessory preview provenance

These are approximate configurator preview assets, not production print files or guarantees of exact physical appearance.

- `heart.png`, `money.png`, `gun.png`: generated transparent illustrations authorized by the user's direct instruction to generate missing product imagery. No authentic images of these accessories were supplied.
- `dog-reference.png`, `dog-white-reference.png`, `dog-spotted-reference.png`: transparent cutouts prepared with image assistance from the three supplied dog screenshots. Source shape, color and markings were requested to be preserved; extraction may differ slightly from the physical product.
- `cat-reference.png`, `cat-black-reference.png`, `cat-orange-reference.png`, `cat-white-reference.png`: individual cutouts prepared with image assistance from the supplied group photo. Same approximation limitation applies.
- `dog.png`, `cat.png`: initial generated prototypes, retained for provenance. They are not selected in the product catalog.

The original supplied screenshots are copied into `artifacts/upgrade/references/`. No existing character PNG, mask, vector, alignment metadata or supplier background was edited. Pet variants all use the confirmed single pet price of 150 MKD; other accessories each use 70 MKD. Exact accessory combinations/fulfillment remain subject to client confirmation.
