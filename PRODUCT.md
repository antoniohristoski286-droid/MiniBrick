# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, and Lucide icons, as specified by the user.

## Users
People in Macedonia choosing a personal gift for a partner, family member, or friend. They want to see how a framed keepsake could reflect their story before placing an order.

## Product Purpose
MiniBrick.mk creates personalized framed gifts with small brick-style human figures, backgrounds, names, dates, and messages. The demo should help a visitor discover the gift and configure an approximate live preview.

## Operating Context
Visitors browse occasions and customize a frame on mobile and desktop. The final order interaction is demonstrative.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Five-step live configurator, occasion presets, visual character styles, backgrounds, personalized copy, order summary, and demo modal. No payment, authentication, database, inventory, admin, backend, or real checkout.

## Brand Commitments
Macedonian customer copy; supplied MiniBrick.mk heart logo and product reference. Deep red, white, warm off-white, black, sentimental boutique character. No LEGO branding.

## Evidence on Hand
Logo and product reference images supplied by the user. Testimonials must be labeled as examples. No real customer numbers, reviews, prices, address, phone, or business legal information were provided.

## Product Principles
- Show the meaning of the gift within seconds.
- Let every configuration change update the preview immediately.
- Make the physical frame feel handcrafted and premium.
- Keep all illustrative content honest about being a demo.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Semantic HTML, labels, keyboard access, visible focus, sufficient contrast, and reduced motion support.
