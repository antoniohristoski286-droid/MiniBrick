# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, and Lucide icons, as specified by the user.

## Users
People in Macedonia choosing a personal gift for a partner, family member, or friend. They want to see how a framed keepsake could reflect their story before placing an order.

## Product Purpose
MiniBrick.mk creates personalized framed gifts with small brick-style human figures, backgrounds, names, dates, and messages. The frontend should help a visitor discover the gift and configure an approximate live preview. The intended product is the real MiniBrick business website; backend development is explicitly deferred by the user.

## Operating Context
Visitors browse occasions and customize a frame on mobile and desktop. The current final step prepares a local request; it does not submit an order.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Five-step configurator, independent modular characters, 110 supplier backgrounds, custom local photos, interactive calendar, white/black frames, accessories, client-side pricing and local copy/JSON order preparation. Preserve these completed features. No payment, authentication, database, inventory, admin, backend, durable photo storage or real order submission is currently implemented.

## Brand Commitments
Macedonian customer copy; supplied MiniBrick.mk heart logo and product reference. Deep red, white, warm off-white, black, sentimental boutique character. No LEGO branding.

## Evidence on Hand
Logo and product reference images supplied by the user. No authentic customer testimonials were supplied; the public page uses factual FAQs instead. The story uses the owner-supplied Instagram caption. Prices, delivery charge and estimated timing were supplied in the upgrade brief. The user supplied the official Instagram profile and its story text; authentic public finished-project photographs are now included. Legal entity and full policy details remain unavailable. Latest confirmed frame rules: 15 × 20 cm for 1–3 figures; exactly four figures use vertical 20 × 25 cm; more than four use horizontal A4 through the numeric 4+ team flow. The browser preview supports 5–24 figures as a technical preview guard, not a business production limit. Both frame colors remain available.

## Product Principles
- Show the meaning of the gift within seconds.
- Let every configuration change update the preview immediately.
- Make the physical frame feel handcrafted and premium.
- Keep preview approximations and the current absence of order submission explicit; never simulate a successful order.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Semantic HTML, labels, keyboard access, visible focus, sufficient contrast, and reduced motion support.
